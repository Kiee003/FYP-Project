### 🎨 AI Customization Guide

**Main file:** `server/deepseekService.js`

| Section | Lines | What It Controls |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| System Prompt | ~35-60 | AI personality & behavior |
| Temperature | ~62-70 | Creativity (0.9 current) |
| `createDetailedPrompt()` | ~75-115 | What questions AI answers |
| `generateDynamicRecommendations()` | ~150-250 | Action items & thresholds |
| `getIntelligentFallback()` | ~300-380 | Fallback messages |

**To customize:** Change the prompt text, temperature (0.3-1.0), max_tokens (300-2000), or severity thresholds.
